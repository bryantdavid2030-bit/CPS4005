## Who we are

Humn Sprt Ltd ("we", "us") operates the Humn Sprt mobile application
and the website humnsprt.com.

## What we collect

- **Account information**: name, email, optional phone number.
- **Enquiry details**: the coach you're enquiring about, your message,
  your organisation (if provided).
- **Diagnostics**: anonymised crash reports and performance traces via
  Sentry.

We do **not** collect location, advertising identifiers, browsing
history, or health data.

## How we use it

- To respond to your enquiries and connect you with practitioners.
- To maintain and improve the reliability of the app.
- To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third-party
advertisers. We use Sentry as a subprocessor for crash reporting under
a standard data-processing agreement.

## Your rights

- Request a copy of your data.
- Request deletion.
- Withdraw consent at any time.

Contact: privacy@humnsprt.com.

## Retention

Enquiry records are retained for up to 24 months, then anonymised.
Diagnostic data is retained for 90 days.

## Changes

We will notify you in-app of material changes before they take effect.
