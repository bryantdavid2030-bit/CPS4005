# App Store Privacy "Nutrition Label" — Humn Sprt

Use these answers in App Store Connect > App Privacy.

## Data collected

### Contact info
- **Name** — Yes, linked to user, for App Functionality (enquiry handling)
- **Email address** — Yes, linked to user, for App Functionality
- **Phone number** — Yes, linked to user (optional), for App Functionality

### User content
- **Customer support** — Yes, linked to user, for App Functionality
  (the message body of enquiries)

### Identifiers
- **User ID** — Yes, linked to user, for App Functionality

### Diagnostics
- **Crash data** — Yes, not linked to user, for App Functionality
  (Sentry)
- **Performance data** — Yes, not linked to user, for Analytics
  (Sentry performance)

## Data NOT collected

- Location
- Health & fitness data
- Financial info
- Browsing history
- Purchases
- Search history
- Advertising data
- Sensitive info
- Contacts
- Photos or videos (unless the user explicitly uploads one)
- Audio data

## Tracking

Humn Sprt does **not** track users across apps or websites owned by
other companies. No third-party SDKs are used for advertising.

## Data retention

- Enquiry data is retained for up to 24 months then anonymised.
- Crash reports are retained for 90 days.
